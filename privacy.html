gmail-mcp privacy policy

gmail-mcp is a private tool used only by its owner, Jeremy Andreani. It is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

The app accesses only the owner's own Gmail account, through Google's official Gmail API, using read and compose scopes. It reads messages and creates drafts in that one account and nothing else.

All data the app handles stays on the owner's own computer. It is not shared, sold, or sent to any third party.

No other users' data is collected, because there are no other users.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: jeremy.andreani@gmail.com
